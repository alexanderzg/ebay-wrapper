# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

## eBay Wrapper

eBay Wrapper is an application used to access and manage eBay listing
information through the eBay API.

The application may access information provided by eBay that is necessary
to retrieve and manage listings authorized by the user.

At this time, eBay Wrapper does not persistently store personal eBay user
data.

eBay Wrapper does not sell, rent, or share personal information with
third parties.

Access to eBay account information is authorized by the user through
eBay's OAuth authorization process.

Users may revoke the application's access to their eBay account through
their eBay account settings.
